This website is entirely self-contained in this one HTML file (except for some images), just because! (Please don't look under the hood, I'm begging you. I barely know how to write HTML and CSS, let alone integrate Javascript with it and make it look pretty and organized >-<)

Some of this website's theming, along with my name, is inspired by the poem Ozymandias (which is very googlable) (from Breaking Bad (I think, I've never seen Breaking Bad)) by Percy Bysshe Shelly. If you like his style and are looking for a more philisophical treatise, I recommend reading his more formal essay, Essay on the Devil and Devils. About Me

My name is Ozymandias, or Ozy for short. I am a casual programmer/tinkerer and I've made some neat stuff! Most of my public projects utilize Python, and I have a handful related to the Nintendo game Miitopia.

Outside of programming, I enjoy running & walking, taking photos, listening to music, parks, exploring all nooks and crannies (on the internet and in real life), fantasy/fiction novels, D&D, Super Smash Bros Ultimate (I main King Dedede and Mii Swordfighter), Satanic/devilish symbols; talismen; and tokens, the Nintendo 3DS & Wii U, general Nintendo homebrewing, Super Mario & Legend of Zelda (shoutout to their orchestral soundtracks), rudamentary video editing, psychological movies, and tinkering with all sorts of computers. I've installed and used a handful of popular linux distros (Arch, Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora), and i can proudly proclaim that Fedora is the best distro and KDE Plasma is the best desktop enviornment.
